# Professional Experience at LIDS Medical

**Dimitrios Birmpas — Medical Physics, Medical Technology and Business Development**

My professional experience at LIDS Medical formed an important bridge between academic Medical Physics and the commercial and clinical reality of medical technology. The role involved working with medical laser systems and energy-based technologies, while developing a practical understanding of how clinics evaluate, adopt and use sophisticated equipment.

## Understanding technology beyond specifications

A medical device cannot be assessed only by its headline parameters. Effective evaluation requires an understanding of physical principles, clinical indications, operator requirements, patient profiles, safety considerations, service support and the economics of use.

My physics and Medical Physics background supported a structured approach to these questions. Technical information had to be translated into practical value for physicians and clinics: what the technology does, where it fits, what distinguishes it, what training it requires and what conditions are necessary for consistent utilisation.

## Clinical communication and education

Medical-device work depends on clear communication with clinicians and healthcare professionals. A useful product presentation is not simply promotional. It must connect the technology with the clinician’s actual workflow and explain both capabilities and limitations accurately.

Demonstrations, training discussions and ongoing support also reveal how different each clinic can be. Two practices may own similar equipment but achieve different outcomes because of differences in patient communication, protocol organisation, team confidence, scheduling and follow-up.

## Commercial development with scientific grounding

The experience also strengthened my work in business development. Building a sustainable technology proposal involves more than completing a sale. It requires an assessment of service demand, competitive position, investment size, running costs, expected utilisation and the clinic’s ability to integrate the service.

This perspective later became central to DB Clinic Development. Equipment ROI is not created by the device alone. It depends on a complete system that includes positioning, pricing, staff training, patient journey, performance monitoring and continuous improvement.

## Lasting professional value

LIDS Medical helped develop a combined professional identity across science, technology and commercial implementation. It provided first-hand experience of the questions clinics ask before investing and of the challenges they face after installation.

That experience continues to inform my work when reviewing medical technologies and supporting dermatology, plastic-surgery and aesthetic clinics. The aim is always to align the technology with clinical purpose, operational capacity and measurable business objectives.
