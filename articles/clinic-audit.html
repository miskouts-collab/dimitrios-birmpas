Clinic Audit Framework

A clinic audit reveals how the patient journey and internal workflows actually operate, not how they are assumed to operate.

The assessment follows the entire pathway from telephone response and booking through reception, consultation, treatment, payment, follow-up and rebooking. It also examines scheduling, staff roles, documentation, stock and equipment utilisation.

Evidence and priorities

Useful evidence includes response rate, waiting time, cancellations, no-shows, consultation conversion, average treatment value and repeat bookings. Findings are prioritised according to safety, operational impact, financial impact and implementation effort.

From findings to action

The output should define the current workflow, specific gaps, probable causes, corrective actions, responsible owners, KPIs and review dates. This turns daily pressure into a measurable improvement programme.
