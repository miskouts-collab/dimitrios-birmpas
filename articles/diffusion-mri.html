Diffusion MRI and the Study of Image Noise

The 2017 postgraduate work of Dimitrios Birmpas examined noise in Diffusion-Weighted Magnetic Resonance Imaging.

Diffusion-Weighted Imaging is sensitive to the microscopic movement of water molecules and provides information beyond anatomical appearance. Its quantitative value depends strongly on signal quality and the statistical behaviour of noise.

Why noise matters

At stronger diffusion weighting the measured signal decreases. When signal approaches the noise floor, calculated diffusion parameters may become biased. Understanding this behaviour is essential for reliable interpretation of diffusion measurements and Apparent Diffusion Coefficient maps.

Scientific continuity

The same principle guides later work in medical technology: a measurement or device should be understood through evidence, uncertainty, limitations, safety and real-world performance.
