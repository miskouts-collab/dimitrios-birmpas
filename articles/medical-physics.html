Medical Physics and Modern Clinical Technology

Medical Physics connects scientific measurement with the safe and effective use of technology in healthcare.

A medical physicist evaluates technology through measurable parameters such as image quality, noise, dose, accuracy, system performance and safety. The role is not limited to knowing how equipment operates; it translates physical principles into clinically meaningful decisions.

Technology assessment before investment

A complete assessment considers scientific validity, clinical indications, operator training, quality controls, maintenance, consumables, workflow impact and realistic utilisation. Technical capability alone does not guarantee clinical or commercial value.

The operational connection

Modern technology performs consistently only within a defined system of protocols, staff responsibilities, patient selection, documentation and follow-up. Dimitrios Birmpas connects Medical Physics with clinic operations so that technology, teams and measurable outcomes are evaluated together.
