# Professional Experience at BTL Hellas Aesthetics

**Dimitrios Birmpas — Medical Technology, Aesthetic Medicine and Clinic Development**

My experience at BTL Hellas Aesthetics expanded my work with advanced energy-based technologies and deepened my understanding of the fast-evolving medical-aesthetics market. The role combined technical knowledge, professional education, commercial development and regular contact with physicians and clinics evaluating new treatment capabilities.

## Evaluating aesthetic technologies in context

The medical-aesthetics sector introduces new systems and applications rapidly. Meaningful evaluation therefore requires a disciplined framework. Technology should be considered in relation to its physical mechanism, intended use, clinical evidence, patient selection, operator requirements, safety, treatment time and fit with the clinic’s existing services.

For a clinic, purchasing equipment is also a strategic decision. A system may be technically strong but still be a poor investment when there is no clear service positioning, insufficient demand, weak team adoption or an unrealistic financial model.

## From equipment installation to service development

A new device becomes valuable only when it becomes a well-designed clinical service. That transition requires:

- clear treatment protocols and responsibilities;
- staff education and confidence;
- appropriate scheduling and patient preparation;
- consistent consultation and expectation management;
- pricing and package design;
- follow-up and repeat-treatment pathways;
- measurement of utilisation, revenue and patient response.

Working closely with the market made this operational gap increasingly visible. Clinics do not merely need equipment; they need a method for integrating technology into everyday practice.

## Communication with healthcare professionals

The role required translating technical information into concise and clinically relevant explanations. Physicians need accuracy, but they also need clarity about workflow, patient suitability, treatment combinations and implementation. Effective professional communication respects both scientific evidence and the practical constraints of a busy clinic.

This experience strengthened my ability to connect different perspectives: the technical perspective of the system, the clinical perspective of the physician, the operational perspective of the team and the commercial perspective of the business.

## Influence on DB Clinic Development

The knowledge developed through BTL Hellas Aesthetics contributed directly to the philosophy of DB Clinic Development. Clinic growth should not be separated from operations. Sustainable development is created when technology selection, team training, patient journey, service design and performance measurement support one another.

Today, this integrated perspective is applied through clinic audits, SOP design, KPI reviews, staff development, equipment assessment and business-development projects for dermatology, plastic-surgery and aesthetic clinics.
